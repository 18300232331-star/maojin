Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you send us

What we collect

When you send an enquiry through this website we collect the details you provide: your name, phone / WhatsApp number, email address (optional), product category and the content of your enquiry.

Why we collect it

Your information is used for one purpose only: to reply to your enquiry and issue quotations. Our sales team may contact you by email, phone or WhatsApp about the products you asked for.

We do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties for marketing. Information is seen only by our own sales staff.

Enquiry records are kept while they are commercially relevant. You may request a copy or deletion of your data at any time by emailing zzq@huosidun.com.cn.
