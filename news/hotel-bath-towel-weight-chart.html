How Much Does a Hotel Bath Towel Weigh? GSM × Size Weight Chart

2026-11-18 · Product Knowledge

A hotel bath towel weighs between about 314 g and 588 g, depending on its size and GSM. The weight is simple arithmetic: GSM is grams per square metre, so weight equals GSM multiplied by the towel's area. A 70×140 cm bath towel covers 0.98 square metres, so at 600 GSM it weighs 588 g, and at 400 GSM it weighs 392 g. Work the weight out before you order — it decides your laundry load, your freight cost and how the towel feels in the guest's hands.

1. The formula: GSM × area = weight

GSM means grams per square metre of fabric. A towel's weight is therefore its GSM multiplied by its area in square metres. To get the area, multiply length by width in metres: a 70×140 cm bath towel is 0.70 m × 1.40 m, which is 0.98 square metres. Multiply 0.98 by the GSM and you have the weight in grams.

Three things follow from that formula. First, size and GSM matter equally: a bigger towel at a lower GSM can weigh the same as a smaller towel at a higher GSM. Second, a small change in GSM is a real change in weight — 100 GSM on a bath towel is about 98 g, which a guest can feel. Third, you can check any supplier's weight claim in seconds with the same multiplication, which makes the formula the fastest way to catch a wrong specification before you order.

The weights in this article use the sizes published on this site — a 70×140 cm bath towel, an 80×160 cm bath sheet and a 34×76 cm face towel — and the GSM values this site publishes, 320, 400, 500 and 600. If you order a different size, run the same multiplication with your own numbers.

Read the table by row first, then by column. Along a row, the weight climbs with GSM: a bath towel goes from 314 g at 320 GSM to 588 g at 600 GSM, nearly double. Down a column, the weight climbs with size: at 500 GSM, a face towel is 129 g, a bath towel is 490 g and a bath sheet is 640 g.

The bath towel row is the one most buyers use. At 400 GSM a 70×140 cm bath towel weighs 392 g — light enough to dry quickly, heavy enough to feel solid. At 600 GSM the same towel weighs 588 g — noticeably plusher, slower to dry, and heavier in the laundry. Neither is better in general; the right GSM depends on your laundry turnaround and the feel you want the guest to notice. Our GSM guide explains what 400, 500 and 600 GSM mean in feel and drying time, and our hotel towel sizes guide lists the sizes this site sells.

The bath sheet row is for suites, spas and pool areas, where guests want more coverage. An 80×160 cm bath sheet at 500 GSM weighs 640 g — heavier than a 600 GSM bath towel — so order bath sheets only where the extra size will be noticed, not across every room. The face towel row shows why small pieces are cheap to get wrong in quantity and cheap to get right: a face towel is only 83–155 g, so a wrong size costs little, but a property that orders two sizes by mistake still has to sort them.

3. What the weight means in the guest's hands

Weight is the first thing a guest feels, before absorbency or softness. A 588 g bath towel feels substantial and luxurious; a 314 g bath towel feels light and dries fast. Guests judge a towel in the first two seconds of picking it up, and the weight sets that judgement. That is why the weight matters as a buying decision, not just as a shipping number.

Heavier is not always better. A heavy towel holds more water, which is good for drying a guest — and bad for drying the towel. A 600 GSM bath towel takes longer to dry than a 400 GSM one, which matters in a humid climate, in a property with a tight laundry turnaround, or in rooms without strong airflow. A lighter towel that dries fully between guests is better than a heavier towel that is still damp at check-in.

Match the weight to the room. Put the heavier weight where guests notice it most — the bath towel — and a lighter, faster-drying weight on hand and face towels, which are changed and washed most often. Keep one GSM per piece across the whole property, so replacements match and housekeeping does not have to sort two nearly identical towels. CottonWorks, the industry resource from Cotton Incorporated, explains the same trade-off in general terms: higher GSM means a denser, more absorbent towel, while lower GSM dries faster.

4. What the weight means for laundry and freight

Weight adds up across a property. Take a 40-room hotel where each room normally takes two bath towels, and apply par stock: one set in the room, one set in the laundry, one set clean and ready on the shelf — three pars, 240 bath towels in circulation. At 600 GSM those 240 towels weigh 141 kg; at 400 GSM they weigh 94 kg. That 47 kg difference is a laundry load, a drying time and a handling effort, every single day.

Freight works the same way. A shipment of 500 bath towels at 600 GSM weighs 294 kg of towels alone, before cartons and pallets; the same 500 towels at 400 GSM weigh 196 kg. The difference — 98 kg — is real freight money on a sea shipment, and it is worth knowing before you fix the GSM, not after the goods are on the water. Our guide on how we pack 500 bath towels for sea freight shows how the towels are packed for shipping; work the weight out first, and the packing and the freight quote both follow from it.

Two practical rules follow. First, fix the GSM before you ask for a freight quote, because the quote depends on the weight. Second, if laundry turnaround is tight, choose the lighter GSM and spend the saving on more pieces per room, rather than choosing the heaviest towel and then running short of dry towels. A property that never runs out of dry towels beats a property with heavier towels and empty shelves.

5. How to order: work the weight out, then send the inquiry

Start from your rooms, not from a catalogue. Count the rooms, the pieces per room, and the pars you run — one set in the room, one in the laundry, one clean on the shelf is the usual three pars. Multiply rooms × pieces per room × pars for each piece, then multiply by the weight from the table above. That gives you the weight in circulation and the weight per shipment, and both numbers belong in your inquiry.

Then send the supplier the size, the GSM and the quantity per piece, and ask for the weight of the exact towel you will receive. The supplier's answer should match the table above for the same size and GSM; if it does not, ask why before you confirm. Orders on this site start from a US$500 minimum including samples, and the per-colour quantity is confirmed at inquiry — send the sizes, the GSM values and the quantities together, and the quotation follows from them.

One last check before you confirm: ask how the weight changes after the first washes. A new towel and a towel washed 50 times do not weigh exactly the same, and the feel changes with washing. Knowing the washed weight up front tells you what the guest will actually hold, not just what left the factory.

FAQ

How do I work out a towel's weight from GSM and size? Multiply length by width in metres to get the area, then multiply the area by the GSM. A 70×140 cm bath towel is 0.98 square metres, so at 500 GSM it weighs 490 g. The table in this article does the arithmetic for the sizes and GSM values this site sells.

Is a heavier towel always better? No. A heavier towel feels plusher and absorbs more, but it dries more slowly and costs more to launder and ship. A 600 GSM bath towel weighs 588 g against 392 g at 400 GSM — the extra 196 g is real laundry and freight cost. Choose the weight that fits your laundry turnaround and your guests, not the heaviest number.

How much do 500 bath towels weigh for shipping? At 600 GSM, 500 bath towels at 70×140 cm weigh 294 kg before cartons and pallets; at 400 GSM the same 500 towels weigh 196 kg. Fix the GSM before you ask for a freight quote, because the quote depends on the weight.

Does a bath sheet weigh much more than a bath towel? Yes. An 80×160 cm bath sheet covers 1.28 square metres against 0.98 for a bath towel, so at the same 500 GSM it weighs 640 g against 490 g. Order bath sheets only for suites, spas and pool areas, where guests notice the extra size.

What weight should hand and face towels be? Lighter than the bath towel, because they are changed and washed most often. A 34×76 cm face towel weighs 103 g at 400 GSM and 129 g at 500 GSM. Put the heavier weight where guests notice it most — the bath towel — and a faster-drying weight on the small pieces.

Do towels weigh the same after washing? Not exactly. A towel's weight and feel change with washing, so ask the supplier for the washed weight as well as the new weight. The table in this article gives the new weight from GSM and size; the washed weight is confirmed at inquiry.
