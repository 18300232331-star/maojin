GSM Explained: What 400, 500 and 600 Really Mean for a Towel

2026-09-18 · Product Knowledge

One term every towel buyer meets is GSM — grams per square metre. 400, 500, 600: a hundred grams apart, and the price moves with it, yet many buyers are unsure what the number really says. GSM decides thickness, absorbency and durability, and with them, cost. This guide explains GSM in plain terms and suggests the right weight for home, hotel and gym use.

Turned into buying actions, three points matter most. First, start from the use case: home buyers prize hand feel and looks, hotels prize wash durability and consistency, gift buyers prize packaging and custom finish — the priority of every spec changes with the scene. Second, write the specs into the order: size, weight, material and process, item by item, and keep a sealed sample so nothing rests on verbal understanding. Third, trial a small batch before scaling up; real washing and use feedback verify quality better than any brochure.

Daily care decides lifespan just as much. Wash new cotton towels once before first use to remove loose lint; avoid washing with zips and hook-and-loop items that snag loops; go easy on softener, which coats fibres and cuts absorbency. Dry in moving air and shade where possible — long harsh sun makes cotton brittle and fades colour. Follow these, and loft and absorbency last far longer.
