Hotel Towel Minimum Order Quantity: MOQ by Product and How to Plan Your First Order

2026-11-20 · Product Knowledge

The minimum order for hotel towels is US$500, including samples — and on top of that, each product has a per-colour minimum: bath towels 300 pieces per colour, hotel-series towels 500 pieces per colour, bath mats and bathrobes 200–300 pieces per colour, and any custom-dyed product 500 pieces per colour. Plan the order so both numbers are met, and the first order goes through without a revision.

1. The two numbers that decide a first order

There are two minimums, and they work together. The first is the order minimum: US$500 for the whole order, including samples, with no exceptions — not even for a sample-only order. The second is the per-colour MOQ: the smallest quantity of one product in one colour that can be made. An order has to clear both. An order of 300 bath towels in one colour clears the per-colour MOQ, but whether it clears US$500 is confirmed on the quotation — the price decides that part, and prices are quoted per order, not listed here.

Why per colour? Because colour is where the production run is set. Each colour is dyed, woven and finished as its own run, and a run below the MOQ costs more to set up than the towels are worth. That is why the count is per colour and not per order: two colours of the same towel are two runs, and each one has to meet the MOQ on its own. The hotel towel sizes guide and the GSM guide cover what the sizes and GSM numbers mean; this article covers how many to order.

2. MOQ by product

ProductMinimum order quantity (per colour)
Bath towel300 pieces
Hotel series towels500 pieces
Bath mat200–300 pieces
Bathrobe200–300 pieces
Any custom-dyed product500 pieces

A few notes on the table. The hotel series is the hotel's own range — the towels made for hotel use in volume — and its 500-piece MOQ is the highest in the table, because it is a full production run. The bath mat and bathrobe range of 200–300 pieces is a range, not a fixed number: the exact figure inside that range depends on the style and is confirmed at inquiry. Custom dyeing — any product dyed to your colour rather than a stock colour — is 500 pieces per colour, because a custom dye run is set up for that colour alone. Face towels and hand towels are not in the table: their MOQ is confirmed at inquiry.

The GSM and size do not change the MOQ. A 600 GSM bath towel and a 400 GSM bath towel both carry the 300-piece per-colour minimum, and a 70×140 cm towel and an 80×160 cm bath sheet both count the same way. What changes the MOQ is the product and the colour — nothing else.

3. Why the US$500 minimum exists

The US$500 minimum covers the order as a whole, and it includes samples. A sample order is still an order: the towels are made, packed and shipped, and the cost of handling a very small order is the same whether it is 10 pieces or 100. The minimum keeps small orders workable for both sides — the buyer gets a real order with real packing and documents, and the factory does not run a production line for an order that costs more to process than it earns.

It also applies to every order, including a first trial and including a sample-only request. There is no small-order exception, and no "just a few pieces to test" order below the minimum. If you need to test the product first, the way to do it is inside the minimum: order the sample quantity that fits the US$500, check it against the five checks before you approve a towel sample, and then place the bulk order on the same specification.

4. Planning examples

Three examples show how the two numbers work together. All of them are in pieces — the US$500 check is confirmed on the quotation, because the price is quoted per order.

One product, one colour. 300 bath towels in white. That clears the bath-towel MOQ of 300 per colour in one line. It is the simplest first order there is.

One product, two colours. 300 white bath towels plus 300 beige bath towels — 600 pieces in total. Each colour clears its own 300-piece MOQ. Ordering two colours does not let you split one 300-piece run into two halves of 150; each colour needs its own 300.

A mixed room set. 300 bath towels in one colour, plus 200 bath mats in the same colour. The towels clear their MOQ and the mats fall inside their 200–300 range. A mixed order like this still has to clear US$500 in total, and the exact mat MOQ inside the 200–300 range is confirmed at inquiry.

A common mistake is to plan by total pieces and forget the per-colour rule. An order of 500 bath towels sounds large, but split across five colours it is 100 pieces per colour — below the 300-piece MOQ five times over. Plan by colour first, then add the colours up.

5. What to send at inquiry

A first-order inquiry has five lines: the product, the colour or colours, the quantity per colour, the size and GSM, and the delivery you need. The size and GSM come from the sizes guide and the GSM guide — 70×140 cm at 600 GSM is the heaviest standard bath towel on this site, and 80×160 cm is the bath sheet. The quantity per colour is where the MOQ table applies: check each line against the table before you send it, and the quotation comes back without a correction.

If you are stocking a property rather than placing a single order, read the wholesale hotel towel sets par-stock guide as well — it covers how many towels a hotel holds per room, which is a different question from the minimum order, and the two together give the full first order: the par stock tells you how many you need, and this article tells you the smallest quantity you can order them in.

FAQ

What is the minimum order for hotel towels? US$500 for the whole order, including samples, with no exceptions. On top of that, each product has a per-colour MOQ — bath towels 300 pieces per colour, hotel series 500, bath mats and bathrobes 200–300, custom-dyed products 500.

Does the US$500 minimum include samples? Yes. Samples count toward the US$500. There is no sample-only order below the minimum.

Can I order 150 white and 150 beige bath towels? No — that is 150 pieces per colour, below the 300-piece bath-towel MOQ. Each colour needs its own 300 pieces.

Does GSM change the MOQ? No. A 600 GSM towel and a 400 GSM towel both carry the same per-colour MOQ. The product and the colour decide the MOQ, not the GSM or the size.

What is the MOQ for face towels and hand towels? Confirm at inquiry. They are not in the table in this article, and the figure is quoted with the order.

Can I mix bath towels and bath mats in one order? Yes, as long as each product clears its own per-colour MOQ and the order total clears US$500. A 300-piece bath towel line plus a 200-piece bath mat line is a normal mixed order.
